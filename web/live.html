Alerts are published as CAP 1.2 on an Atom feed, the format NDMA's SACHET carries and every alerting aggregator already parses. Point a reader at /api/alerts/feed.atom, or read the same alerts as JSON at /api/alerts.
We publish; we do not broadcast to the public. That last mile belongs to IMD and NDMA, and this prototype should never compete with the warning people are meant to act on.
Detection and intensity are read from INSAT-3D/3DR/3DS and GridSat-B1 infrared imagery; forecasts are cross-validated on storms never seen in training, with 95% intervals from resampling whole storms. Known limits are written down in limitations.md rather than left for you to find.
